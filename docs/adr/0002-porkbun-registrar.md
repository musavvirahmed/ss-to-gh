# Porkbun as Registrar

`musavvir.info` can leave Squarespace hosting while staying registered there (DNS-only), and Porkbun’s listed `.info` renewal (USD 22.14/yr) is slightly higher than Squarespace’s catalog domain renewal (~USD 20/yr). **Decision: transfer the domain to Porkbun as Registrar** so `musavvir.info` sits with `musavvir.work` and `musavvir.fyi`. Rejected: DNS-only at Squarespace. Mail on the name is nice-to-have; brief breakage during cutover is acceptable.
